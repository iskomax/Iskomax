Questions

Do you need my password?

No. We only need your Player ID. Never share your password with anyone.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within a few minutes. Contact support if yours takes longer than an hour.

What if I enter the wrong ID?

Check the ID before paying. Diamonds sent to a wrong ID usually can't be recovered.
